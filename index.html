Quick constants

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Which tax brackets are used in the Income Tax Calculator?

    The calculator uses the 6-tier progressive schedule (0% up to ₱250,000; 20% excess over ₱250k; ₱30,000 + 25% over ₱400k; ₱130,000 + 30% over ₱800k; ₱490,000 + 32% over ₱2M; and ₱2,410,000 + 35% over ₱8M) and shows both the Basic Tax and Excess Rate Tax.

    How do live currency exchange rates work offline?

    When online, the converter fetches fresh USD-based market rates and caches them in localStorage. If you open the page offline, it automatically falls back to your last saved rates or built-in reference rates.

    Why is the looping input capped at n = 170?

    In standard IEEE-754 double-precision JavaScript numbers, 170! is the largest factorial before exceeding Number.MAX_VALUE and overflowing to Infinity.

    Is any income or payroll data sent to a server?

    No. All unit conversions, tax computations, loop traces, and payroll records run locally in your browser memory and disappear as soon as you clear them or close the tab.

    Quad Reckoner

    Runs entirely in your browser. Only the exchange-rate lookup goes online, and it sends none of your inputs.
